Pizza Delivery in Aruba: Wood-Fired Pizza to Your Door

Che Bar Team · June 3, 2026 · 5 min read

You spent the day at the beach. You're sunburned, sandy, and happy. The last thing you want to do is get dressed and go out for dinner. Good news — we deliver.

There's a moment on every Aruba vacation where you just want to stay in. Maybe you're back at the hotel after a full day of snorkeling. Maybe you're at your Airbnb with a killer sunset view from the balcony. Maybe the kids are wiped out and nobody wants to go anywhere.

That's when pizza delivery becomes the best decision of the trip.

At Che Bar, we make wood-fired Neapolitan pizza — proper dough, quality ingredients, cooked in a real pizza oven with that blistered crust and smoky flavor. And you can have it delivered straight to your hotel room, Airbnb, or villa through two delivery apps that operate across the island.

Both apps deliver across the island — Palm Beach, Noord, Eagle Beach, Oranjestad, and beyond. Delivery times vary, but you're generally looking at 30–45 minutes from order to door.

Tip: If you're staying at a high-rise hotel, let the front desk know you have a delivery coming. Some hotels have a designated pickup area in the lobby. If you're at an Airbnb, make sure your address and any gate codes are in the delivery notes.

Why Order Delivery

Look — you're on an island with incredible restaurants, and we'd love to see you at Che Bar in person. But some nights, delivery just makes more sense:

You're at an Airbnb with a view. Grab a bottle of wine from the supermarket, order a couple of pizzas, and eat on the balcony while the sun goes down. That's a $50 dinner for four people with a view you'd pay $200 for at a resort.

The kids are done. They've been in the sun all day. They're cranky. Nobody's putting on shoes. Pizza in the room, movie on the iPad, everyone's happy.

You're sunburned. It happens. Even with SPF 50. Some days you just need to stay in with aloe vera and a Margherita pizza.

It's your last night. You're packing. The suitcase is open on the bed. You don't have time for a sit-down dinner, but you still want something good. Delivery solves it.

A Note About Delivery in Aruba

Food delivery in Aruba is still relatively new compared to what you might be used to back home. There's no Uber Eats or DoorDash here. The two main platforms are VapVap Eats and Aruba To You, and both work well.

A few things to know:

Delivery fees vary depending on the distance from the restaurant. Usually a few dollars.

Tipping the driver is always appreciated. These folks are driving around in the Aruba heat to bring you pizza — a couple of dollars goes a long way.

Pay in the app — both platforms accept credit cards and some local payment methods. You usually don't need cash.
