What Makes Che Bar Special? Breakfast, Pizza & Live Music in Palm Beach

Che Bar Team · May 19, 2026 · 5 min read

We're a little restaurant at Paseo Herencia Mall that opens early, stays open late, and tries to make every meal worth remembering. Here's what we're all about.

People ask us all the time — what is Che Bar, exactly? Are you a breakfast spot? A pizza place? A cocktail bar? The honest answer is: we're all of those things, and that's what makes us a little different.

We open at 7:30 in the morning with Dutch pancakes and fresh coffee, and we're still going strong at night with wood-fired pizza, cocktails, and live music. It's a place that changes vibe throughout the day but always feels welcoming, whether you're rolling in before the beach or closing out the night.

Here's what keeps people coming back.

Morning: Breakfast Worth Waking Up For

Dutch Pancakes

Breakfast

This is what we're probably best known for in the morning. Our Dutch pancakes are thin, wide, and come with a variety of toppings — think Nutella and banana, fresh fruit, bacon and cheese, or simply butter and powdered sugar. They're not like American pancakes at all. Lighter, crispier around the edges, and big enough to share (though most people don't).

If you've never had a Dutch pancake before, this is the place to try one.

The Breakfast Burrito

Breakfast

For something more savory and filling, the breakfast burrito is a guest favorite. It's packed, it's satisfying, and it's the kind of thing that will hold you through a full morning of beach and snorkeling without thinking about food.

The Big Breakfast

Breakfast

When you're really hungry — or you just believe that vacation breakfast should be a proper event — this is the one. Bacon, scrambled eggs, toast, skillet potatoes, and fresh fruit. It's everything you want on a plate, done right, with a good cup of coffee on the side.

Pro tip: Grab a table early. The morning crowd picks up around 9 AM, especially during high season. If you're here by 7:30 or 8, you'll have your pick of seats.

We also do empanadas, fresh juices, and espresso drinks if you're looking for something quicker. Grab and go, or sit down and take your time — no rush either way.

Evening: Neapolitan Pizza & Cocktails

Wood-Fired Neapolitan Pizza

Dinner

At night, the kitchen switches gears and we fire up the pizza oven. Our Neapolitan-style pizzas are made with fresh dough, quality ingredients, and cooked in a proper wood-fired oven the way they should be — blistered crust, melted mozzarella, and that smoky flavor you only get from real fire.

We keep the menu focused. A handful of classic pizzas done really well, rather than a 40-item menu where nothing stands out. When you bite into one, you'll taste the difference.

Cocktails & Happy Hour

Drinks

Our cocktail menu is built for the island — refreshing, well-made, and designed for warm nights. Whether you're into a classic mojito, something tropical, or a cold beer after a day in the sun, we've got you covered.

Happy hour runs daily from 5 to 7 PM with deals on cocktails. It's the perfect way to transition from beach mode to dinner mode — grab a drink, settle in, and figure out what pizza you're ordering.

Local tip: Happy hour plus a pizza is honestly one of the best-value dinners on the Palm Beach strip. You eat well, drink well, and you're right in the middle of everything.

Live Music

Live Performances

Entertainment

This is a big part of what makes Che Bar feel like more than just a restaurant. We have live music regularly — local artists and performers who bring real energy to the space. It's not background noise. It's the kind of music that makes you stay for one more drink, lean back in your chair, and think: yeah, this is why I came to Aruba.

The vibe is relaxed, never too loud, and it fits the evening perfectly. Pizza, a cocktail, and live music under the Palm Beach sky — that's a good night.

The Location

Paseo Herencia Mall, Palm Beach

Location

We're right at Paseo Herencia Mall on the high-rise hotel strip in Palm Beach — the heart of Aruba's tourism area. If you're staying at any of the big hotels along J.E. Irausquin Boulevard, we're probably within walking distance.

Palm Beach is where the action is. The beach is steps away, there are shops and restaurants all around, and at night the whole strip comes alive. It's the most walkable, convenient part of the island — and that's exactly why we set up here.

Whether you're starting your morning before heading to the beach or winding down after a day of exploring, the location makes it easy to stop by without going out of your way.

From Morning to Night

That's really the thing about Che Bar — it works at any time of day. Breakfast before the beach, a quick empanada in the afternoon, happy hour cocktails at sunset, pizza and live music at night. It's not trying to be everything, but it covers the moments that matter.

We're a small team, we love what we do, and we put care into everything that comes out of the kitchen. That's it. No gimmicks, no pretension — just good food, good drinks, and a great spot to enjoy them.

The basics: Open daily from 7:30 AM. Breakfast & coffee all morning. Wood-fired pizza in the evening. Happy hour 5–7 PM. Live music regularly. Paseo Herencia Mall, Palm Beach, Aruba.
