Best Breakfast in Palm Beach, Aruba: Dutch Pancakes, Empanadas & More

Che Bar Team · June 3, 2026 · 6 min read

You're in Aruba, the sun is out, and you need a proper breakfast before hitting the beach. We've been feeding the Palm Beach crowd since 7:30 every morning — here's what to order.

Breakfast in Palm Beach doesn't have to be a $40 hotel buffet. At Che Bar, right at Paseo Herencia Mall on the high-rise strip, we serve breakfast every day from 7:30 AM to 3:00 PM — so whether you're an early riser or more of a sleep-in-on-vacation person, we've got you covered.

We're within walking distance of every major high-rise hotel on J.E. Irausquin Boulevard — Riu, Hilton, Radisson, Holiday Inn, Barcelo — so you don't need a car or a taxi. Just walk over, grab a table, and start the day right.

Here's what's on the breakfast menu.

The Big Breakfast

Big Breakfast $14.00
Most Popular

This is our number one seller, and for good reason. Scrambled eggs, bacon, skillet potatoes, toast, and a side of fresh fruit. It's the kind of breakfast that makes you feel like you're on vacation — everything on one plate, done properly, with a good cup of coffee next to it.

It's filling enough to carry you through a full morning at the beach without thinking about food. And honestly, at $14 for a full cooked breakfast in Palm Beach, it's hard to beat.

Dutch Pancakes

If you've never had a Dutch pancake, you're in for a treat. They're nothing like American pancakes — think thin, wide, and crispy around the edges, more like a cross between a crepe and a pancake. We make them fresh and offer eight different toppings:

Sweet $14.50–$15.75
Sweet

Berries & Whipped Cream — fresh strawberries and blueberries. Banana Dulce de Leche — sliced bananas with Argentine dulce de leche. S'Mores — graham crackers, marshmallows, and chocolate. Apple Pie — sliced apples, raisins, and cinnamon. Peanut Butter & Banana — peanut butter, sliced banana, and honey. Brie, Walnuts & Honey — walnuts inside the pancake, topped with Brie and honey.

Savory $14.75–$15.50
Savory

Bacon & Apple — bacon strips and sliced apples cooked right into the pancake. Farmer's Market — bacon, ham, onions, mushrooms, and melted Gouda cheese. These are the ones locals order.

If you're not sure which to try, the Berries & Whipped Cream is the crowd favorite, and the Farmer's Market is perfect if you want something hearty.

Empanadas

Argentine Empanadas $4.00–$4.50
Quick & Affordable

Our empanadas are one of the things people come back for again and again. Flaky, fluffy pastry with generous fillings — choose from Beef, Chicken, Cheese, or Spinach & Ricotta. They're handmade, they're fresh, and at $4–5 each they're the best quick bite on the strip.

Grab a couple before the beach, or order a few as a side with your coffee. They're also a great option if you're on a budget — two empanadas and a coffee is a solid breakfast for under $15.

Savory

French Toasts & Fruit $11.50
Sweet

Bagels $9.50–$14.75
Savory

Three options: Salmon, Cream Cheese & Capers (the premium pick), Egg, Bacon & Cheese (classic), or Cream Cheese BLT (the best deal at $9.50). All toasted, all solid.

Omelet $14.75
Savory

Choose your meat — ham, bacon, or chorizo — with tomatoes, onions, peppers, mushrooms, and cheese. Comes with toast and skillet potatoes.

Great Coffee

We take our coffee seriously. All our espresso drinks are available hot or iced, and you can add a flavor shot for $1 — hazelnut, caramel, vanilla, coconut, white chocolate, or amaretto.

Americano ($5.00) — strong and clean. Cappuccino ($5.75) — perfectly frothed. Latte ($5.75) — smooth and creamy. Espresso ($3.50) — quick and intense. Plus tea ($4.00) and hot chocolate ($4.50).

Whether you need a quick espresso to go or want to sit down with a flavored iced latte, the coffee here is the real deal.

Yes, We Serve Morning Cocktails

You're on vacation. There are no rules. If you want a mimosa with your Dutch pancake or an espresso martini to kick off the day, we won't judge — we'll make you a good one.

And if you happen to come on a Monday, you're in luck: Mimosa Monday means 2-for-1 mimosas all day. Fresh orange juice, bubbly, and a pancake. That's a pretty good start to the week.

Mimosa Monday: 2-for-1 mimosas every Monday at Che Bar. Breakfast cocktails are not just acceptable — they're encouraged.

Why Breakfast at Che Bar Works

There are a few things that make this spot work for breakfast in Palm Beach:

The location. We're at Paseo Herencia Mall, right on the main strip. If you're staying at any of the high-rise hotels, we're a short walk. No taxi needed, no Uber, no hassle.

The hours. 7:30 AM to 3:00 PM, every day. Whether you're an early bird or a late riser, you won't miss breakfast.

The variety. Dutch pancakes, empanadas, burritos, bagels, omelets, French toast, the Big Breakfast — there's something for everyone, sweet or savory, quick or leisurely.

The prices. A full breakfast, including coffee, for under $20. In Palm Beach. That's good value.

The vibe. It's relaxed. You're not being rushed. Sit down, have your coffee, plan your beach day, and take your time. That's what vacation mornings should feel like.
